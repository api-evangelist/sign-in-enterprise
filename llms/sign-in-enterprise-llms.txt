# Sign In Enterprise

> Sign In Enterprise (formerly Traction Guest, now part of Sign In Solutions) is an enterprise visitor management system (VMS) used by security-conscious organizations across aerospace and defense, government, manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, and technology. Its REST API — "Guest Connect" / the Sign In Solutions VMS API — lets developers retrieve and modify visitor and employee sign-in data to extend workflows: hosts, invites, sign-ins, registrations, locations, capacities, parking, group visits, watchlists, email templates, and audit logs. REST over JSON, OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect scoped access, idempotent writes via an Idempotency-Key header.
